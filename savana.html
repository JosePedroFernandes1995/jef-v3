CHAPTER I — MOZAMBIQUE

SAVANA

An encounter with the ancestral soul of Mozambique, told through clothing, literature and sound.

THE PLACE

Moçam
bique.

SAVANA begins with a landscape — vast, warm, rhythmic and inseparable from the Indian Ocean.

Mozambique becomes more than a visual reference. Its colours, artistic expressions, traditions and cultural encounters become the language through which the collection is constructed.

JEF approaches the country as a meeting point: between nature and architecture, ancestral rhythm and contemporary form, memory and movement.

THE STORY

01

The visual vocabulary of SAVANA moves through some of Mozambique's most distinctive cultural expressions.

The capulana introduces colour and pattern. Mussiro evokes ritual, identity and materiality. The landscape brings the tones and textures of savanna, tropical vegetation and the Indian Ocean.

Artistic and architectural references enter the collection not as decoration, but as fragments of a larger cultural narrative.

The result is a wardrobe conceived as a journey: Portuguese in language, Mozambican in inspiration, and contemporary in expression.
